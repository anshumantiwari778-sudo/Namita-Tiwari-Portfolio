PROFILE

Mathematics, Artificial Intelligence & Research

Prof. Namita Tiwari is a Professor in the Department of Mathematics, School of Basic Sciences, and Dean (Research & Development) at Chhatrapati Shahu Ji Maharaj University, Kanpur.

She holds a Ph.D. in Cryptography from MNNIT Allahabad and an M.Sc. in Pure Mathematics from IIT Kanpur. Her research connects mathematics with cryptography, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, biometrics and emerging digital technologies.
