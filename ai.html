Artificial Intelligence

M.Sc. Mathematics with Artificial Intelligence & Data Science

📖

Course Overview

This course introduces the fundamental concepts of Artificial Intelligence, intelligent agents, problem solving, knowledge representation, reasoning, learning and Natural Language Processing.

UNIT I 🧠

Introduction & Problem Solving

This unit introduces the foundations of Artificial Intelligence, intelligent agents and problem-solving approaches.

  • What is AI?
  • Foundations of AI
  • History of AI
  • Agents and Environments
  • Nature of the Environment
  • Problem-Solving Agents
  • Problem Formulation
  • Search Strategies
UNIT II 🔗

Knowledge & Reasoning

This unit introduces knowledge-based agents, representation, reasoning and logic used in Artificial Intelligence.

  • Knowledge-Based Agents
  • Knowledge Representation
  • Reasoning and Logic
  • Propositional Logic
  • First-Order Logic
  • Using First-Order Logic
  • Inference in First-Order Logic
  • Forward Chaining
  • Backward Chaining
UNIT III 📊

Learning

This unit introduces learning from observations and different approaches to learning in Artificial Intelligence.

  • Learning from Observations
  • Forms of Learning
  • Inductive Learning
  • Learning Decision Trees
  • Why Learning Works
  • Learning in Neural Networks
  • Learning in Belief Networks
UNIT IV 💬

Practical Natural Language Processing

This unit introduces practical applications of Natural Language Processing along with perception, image processing and speech technologies.

  • Practical NLP Applications
  • Efficient Parsing
  • Scaling Up the Lexicon
  • Scaling Up the Grammar
  • Ambiguity
  • Perception
  • Image Formation
  • Image Processing Operations for Early Vision
  • Speech Recognition
  • Speech Synthesis
