Mathematics for Data Science

M.Sc. Mathematics with Artificial Intelligence & Data Science

Course Overview

This course develops the mathematical and logical foundations required for modern techniques in Data Science, Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, covering set theory, functions, differentiation, integration, matrices, determinants, vector spaces and related concepts.

UNIT I 📐

Set Theory & Mathematical Foundations

This unit introduces the mathematical foundations required for Data Science, including sets, functions, relations, recurrence relations and fundamental counting concepts.

  • Binary Operations
  • Functions and Relations
  • Recurrence Relations
  • Generating Functions
  • Permutations and Combinations
  • Pigeon Hole Principle
  • Propositional Calculus
UNIT II ∫

Differentiation & Integration

This unit covers important concepts of differentiation and integration, including partial differentiation, maxima and minima, double integration and change of order of integration.

  • Logarithmic Differentiation
  • Partial Differentiation
  • Homogeneous Functions
  • Maxima and Minima
  • Double Integration
  • Change of Order of Integration
UNIT III ∇

Vectors & Vector Calculus

This unit covers vector operations, scalar and vector fields, vector differentiation and the fundamental concepts of vector integration.

  • Dot Product
  • Cross Product
  • Triple Product
  • Scalar and Vector Fields
  • Vector Differentiation
  • Gradient
  • Divergence
  • Curl
  • Vector Integration
UNIT IV 🔢

Matrices & Determinants

This unit covers matrices and determinants, matrix operations, rank, systems of simultaneous equations, the Cayley-Hamilton theorem, eigenvalues and eigenvectors.

  • Matrix Definitions and Types
  • Addition of Matrices
  • Subtraction of Matrices
  • Multiplication of Matrices
  • Singular and Non-Singular Matrices
  • Rank of a Matrix
  • Solution of Simultaneous Equations
  • Cayley-Hamilton Theorem
  • Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors
